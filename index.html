How clients find you has changed

People now decide who to trust long before they call, visit or book. Here is how that shift happened, and why it matters for your business.

1
Before

Word of mouth

People chose a doctor, a designer, a travel agent or a restaurant because a friend recommended them. Reputation lived offline, and a good sign outside the door was enough.

2
Then

Search and reviews

Google and review sites became the first stop. A website and a few good ratings helped, but every business started to look the same on a results page.

3
Now

Social first

Today people look you up on Instagram before anything else. Your feed, your reels and your website are your first impression. A brand that looks consistent and premium online gets the booking. One that looks ordinary gets skipped.
